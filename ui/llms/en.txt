# Reise-Agent (English) – Verified price attestations

> Independent travel price comparison. We do not sell bookings; we sell
> signed price observations with source and timestamp (x402).
> Every response is machine-verifiable; the recommendation remains the lowest price.

## What the service does

- Compares flights, hotels, ferries and cruises from multiple sources.
- Issues an **Ed25519-signed attestation**: route, date, price,
  price basis, source, observation time, plausibility check.
- The attestation is an **observation**, not a booking or price guarantee.

## Paid endpoint

`POST {{BASE_URL}}/api/attest`

Price: **{{PRICE}} USDC** per attestation (x402 v2, scheme `exact`).
Network: `{{NETWORK}}`.
Asset: `{{ASSET}}` (USDC).
payTo: `{{PAY_TO}}`.

### Flow

1. `POST /api/attest` without payment → `402 Payment Required`
   with a `PAYMENT-REQUIRED` header (JSON: price, network, asset, payTo).
2. Sign the payment with an x402 client (EIP-3009) and repeat the request with
   `PAYMENT-SIGNATURE`.
3. `200 OK` + signed JSON + `PAYMENT-RESPONSE` (transaction hash).

### Request body (example)

```json
{
  "route": "BER-FCO",
  "travel_date": "2026-11-05",
  "price": "129.99",
  "currency": "EUR",
  "price_scope": "per_person",
  "source": "googleflights-local",
  "source_url": "https://www.google.com/travel/flights",
  "passengers": 1
}
```

### Free endpoints

- `POST {{BASE_URL}}/api/attest/validate` – pre-check without payment
- `POST {{BASE_URL}}/api/attest/verify` – verify a signature
- `GET  {{BASE_URL}}/api/attest/key` – public Ed25519 key
- `GET  {{BASE_URL}}/.well-known/agent-payments.json` – discovery
- `GET  {{BASE_URL}}/.well-known/capabilities.json` – capability catalogue (what is payable, what is planned)
- `GET  {{BASE_URL}}/.well-known/x402.json` – x402 manifest (Saylor/Agent Bazaar)
- `GET  {{BASE_URL}}/capabilities` – the same catalogue under a short URL
- `GET  {{BASE_URL}}/llms.txt` – this file

## Discovery

- Payment/service metadata: `/.well-known/agent-payments.json`
- The x402 Bazaar extension is included in the discovery document and in the 402 response.
- No API key, no account, no onboarding.

## Limits

- No booking, no payment for travel, no tour operation.
- No price guarantee; prices may change after the observation.
- No invented ratings or availability.
- Rate limit and refunds: see `/.well-known/agent-payments.json`.
